High-Fidelity Physiological Signal Synthesis Using Generative Adversarial Networks with Conv1DTranspose Generator and InceptionTime Discriminator

Majid Khan & Rahim Khan — Department of Computer Science, AWKUM, Pakistan

This research presents a novel GAN framework for synthesizing realistic physiological signals (ECG, EEG) using a Conv1DTranspose generator paired with an InceptionTime-based discriminator. The approach addresses the critical challenge of limited medical data availability while preserving patient privacy through high-quality synthetic data generation for healthcare AI applications.
